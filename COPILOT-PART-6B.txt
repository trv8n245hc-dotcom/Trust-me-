7. HOW TO ORDER THE PEOPLE WHO ARE ON R0

Most of my floor will be on R0 at the start of a month, so they must not sit in a random heap at
the bottom.

- Earners come first, highest rand at the top, as already described.
- Then everyone on R0, sorted by HOW CLOSE THEY ARE TO QUALIFYING, nearest first.
- Someone who cleared one gate and just missed the other must sit above someone who missed both
  badly. The closer you are, the higher you sit.
- On each of those rows, say in plain words what is standing in their way, for example
  "3 sales short of 31.6%" or "41 contacts short of gate 338".
- Never show a R0 group in random or alphabetical order. The point is that people can see how
  close they are and what to chase.


8. TIES

If two people are on exactly the same rand value, break the tie by points, then by
effectiveness, then by contacts. Never let the order jump around between refreshes.


9. DIAL DIRECT DISPLAY

Dial Direct has no effectiveness target yet.

- Do not show "/ 0.0%" next to their effectiveness. It reads like a real target of zero.
  Show "Display only - no target set" instead.
- Never show the word "Disabled" anywhere on the board. If a figure does not apply, show a dash.
  If it does apply, show the real percentage. Their conversion, closing and effectiveness must
  all still calculate and display normally.


10. WHAT THE BOARD SHOWS WHEN NOTHING IS LOADED YET

Since the sample no longer loads by itself, the board will be empty the first time I open it.
Do not show a blank screen or a row of zeros. Show a short friendly panel that says:

   "Nothing loaded yet.
    Step 1 - upload your Organogram, the staff list of who works where.
    Step 2 - upload your daily stats file.
    Or press Load Sample to see how the board works."

with the three buttons right there on that panel.
