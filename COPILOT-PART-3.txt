8. THE SCREENS

THE BIG NUMBER, across the top of every screen.
The live pool value in very large type. This is the number that flexes up and down with how
the teams are doing and it needs to be in big writing. Next to it show how far above or below
the R450,000 base it is, how many days are banked, the participation percentage, the total
allocated so far, and who the top earner is and what they are on. Add a bar showing where the
pool is sitting between its floor and its cap.

TAB 1 - ARENA.
The full leaderboard, ranked first to last. Each row: rank, agent name, their business
manager, a coloured chip for their brand, their team lead, contacts with the gate result and
a little progress bar, effectiveness with the target result and a progress bar, conversion,
closing, their bracket badge, their points, a box to type QA points into, and the rand they
have earned. Let me sort by any column, search for a name, and filter by brand, by team lead,
by business manager, and by date.

TAB 2 - BRANDS AND AVERAGES.
One card per brand showing headcount, the running average contacts, the live contacts gate,
the effectiveness target, the actual brand effectiveness, and how many have qualified. Every
agent must be able to see what the running average contacts are for their brand. Underneath,
let me pick a brand and see every person in it with their actual numbers, ranked on
effectiveness, with the focus on contacts and effectiveness. Each team lead must be able to
select their own brand and their own team.

TAB 3 - MY PATH. Two tools, for whichever agent is selected.

  THE COACH - a fun, educational calculator. Remember that conversion is leads out of
  contacts, closing is sales out of the leads created, and effectiveness is sales divided by
  contacts taken. Write it in a friendly sentence, for example:
     "From your 344 contacts, at your current closing of 75.0%, you need 18 more leads -
      that is 13 more sales - to reach 31.6% effectiveness."
  If the agent is on zero it must still work and tell them what they need. Add sliders for
  contacts, leads and sales that instantly recalculate conversion, closing and effectiveness.

  THE PATH LOADER - a box and slider from R0 to R100,000 where the agent types how much they
  need to earn to be comfortable, and a "Load Path" button. When they press it, work backwards
  to find the numbers that would pay them that amount against today's actual field, then
  update contacts, leads, sales, conversion, closing and effectiveness on the board
  automatically, each showing what it was before. Put a clear banner across the top saying
  "PROJECTION - this is what your projection would look like if you achieved the following"
  with a button to go back to the real numbers. Show the rand value they are aiming at in very
  big writing, along with the rank it would put them at.
  Be honest with them in three situations:
     exact hit    - "Path found - hit these numbers and you land here."
     overshoot    - "Nearest achievable point above your target. Earnings step up in jumps,
                     at each bracket edge and again when you break into the Super Club."
     out of reach - "Out of reach today: 18 agents are already sharing this pool. Your
                     ceiling against the current field is R29,747 - it rises as others fall
                     away and as the pool grows."

TAB 4 - RULES.
Every number above must be editable here, on the page, with the board recalculating
instantly: the pool base, the cap, the floor, the daily 1.5%, the individual R100,000 cap,
the unlock floor, each brand's target and gate percentage and earning on/off switch and
colour, the whole bracket table, the 70/30 weighting, the QA table, the Super Club amounts,
the Brand Champion amount and the Winner's Circle percentages. Save all of it in the browser
so it is still there tomorrow. Give me a button to export the rules to a file and one to
import them, so I can send the exact same setup to my other team leads.
