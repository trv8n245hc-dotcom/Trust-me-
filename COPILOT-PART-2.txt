5. THE PRIZES - ALL PAID OUT OF THE POOL, NEVER ON TOP OF IT

- SUPER CLUB, on bracket points: 1st place R15,000, 2nd place R10,000, 3rd place R8,000.
- BRAND CHAMPION: R5,000 to the top qualifier in each earning brand. This can stack with
  Super Club.
- WINNER'S CIRCLE: if 5 or fewer agents qualify in total, each of them gets an extra 5% to
  10% of the pool value, sliding with how few there are - 5 qualifiers gets 5% each, 1
  qualifier gets 10%. This only applies at 5 or fewer.

Work the money out in this order:
   reserves      = Super Club money + (R5,000 for each earning brand that has a qualifier)
   circle total  = number of qualifiers x pool x winner's circle percentage
   distributable = (pool - reserves - circle total) x unlock factor
   base pay      = the agent's share x distributable
   gross         = base pay + super club + brand champion + winner's circle
   final         = gross minus their QA deduction, never below R0, never above R100,000

BUDGET GUARD: after all of that, if the total of everyone's pay would come to more than the
live pool, scale every single payout down proportionally so it fits. The purpose of a sales
pool is to manage budget, not to exceed it. It must be impossible to blow the budget.
