WHAT IT IS

A daily sales commission leaderboard called the "Sales Agent Arena" for our
HiPPO-branded insurance sales floor. Team leads upload a CSV of yesterday's numbers once a
day. Agents look at the board to see where they stand and what they are earning.

Right now the floor runs a flat commission pool, which pays everyone "on average" and lets
people coast. It is also unfair between brands, because Budget Insurance receives far more
contacts than Auto & General, so Budget agents look better on a shared leaderboard purely
because of call volume. This board replaces that with a bracket system where every target is
measured against the agent's OWN brand.

1. THE FOUR BRANDS

Budget Insurance    - effectiveness target 31.6%  - earns money
First for Women     - effectiveness target 34.7%  - earns money
Auto & General      - effectiveness target 31.5%  - earns money
Dial Direct         - no target set               - DISPLAY ONLY, earns nothing yet

Dial Direct agents must still appear on the board with their contacts, their effectiveness
and their own brand average, but they earn R0 and are excluded from all prizes until a
target is set for them. Ticking a box and typing a target must be enough to switch them on.


2. THE TWO GATES - MISS EITHER ONE AND YOU EARN R0

Gate 1 - CONTACTS, measured against your own brand.
   Work out the brand's average contacts = total contacts in that brand divided by the
   number of agents in that brand. The agent must have at least 90% of that average.
   Recalculate this from the uploaded file every single day.
   The 90% must be editable, separately for each brand.

   This is the most important rule in the whole build. It is what stops a high-volume brand
   beating a low-volume brand for no reason. Every agent is judged against their own channel.

Gate 2 - EFFECTIVENESS, against that brand's target.
   Effectiveness = sales divided by contacts, shown as a percentage.
   The agent must be at or above their brand's target (31.6, 34.7 or 31.5).

If an agent misses EITHER gate, they earn exactly R0. No partial payment. It is a balancing
act - they have to hit both. Show clearly which gate they failed and by how much, in plain
words, for example "8 contacts short of gate 352" or "3 sales short of 31.6%". Keep people
who failed visible on the leaderboard, greyed out, because seeing the gap is what motivates
them.


3. THE BRACKET - HOW MUCH YOU EARN

Effectiveness is the single most important metric in this whole process. It must matter more
than anything else, both in the weighting and in setting the bracket.

For everyone who cleared both gates:
   effectiveness depth = their effectiveness divided by their brand's target
   contacts depth      = their contacts divided by their brand's average contacts
   points              = bracket multiplier x ((0.70 x effectiveness depth) + (0.30 x contacts depth))
   their share of the money = their points divided by the total points of everyone who qualified

Bracket multipliers, based on effectiveness depth:
   Legend     - 130% or more of target - multiplier 2.00
   Elite      - 120% or more of target - multiplier 1.70
   Contender  - 110% or more of target - multiplier 1.45
   Riser      - 105% or more of target - multiplier 1.25
   Entry      - 100% or more of target - multiplier 1.00

The deeper you go into effectiveness, and the deeper you go into contacts, the higher your
earning percentage is than the person below you. That is what sets your place on the
leaderboard - first, second, third, all the way down to last.


4. THE MONEY POOL

The whole cohort shares one pool. The base is R450,000.

- The board opens at R450,000 on day one so agents can see the full opportunity immediately.
  It must NOT start at some small value.
- Every day, work out participation = the number of agents who qualified divided by the
  number of agents in earning brands.
- If today's participation is the same or better than yesterday's, the pool grows by 1.5%.
  If it drops, the pool shrinks by 1.5%. This compounds day after day, so every single day
  counts and the value visibly expires if people fall off.
- The pool may grow above R450,000, but stop it dead at an upper cap of R520,000.
- It may never fall below 70% of the base.
- Store one snapshot per reporting date so the daily growth compounds across the month in
  date order. If I delete a bad upload, the pool must recalculate immediately.

UNLOCK FACTOR: only part of the pool is released, based on participation.
   unlock = 0.35 + (0.65 x participation)
This is what stops three people walking away with the entire R450,000. Make the 0.35
editable.
